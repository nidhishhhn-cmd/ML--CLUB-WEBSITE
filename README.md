### Multi-Page Cinematic Animated Website with Looping Video Background, Interactive Multi-Photo Lightbox Gallery, Access Control, 3D Tilt, Particle Canvas, Module Photo Upload & Online Project Links

## 🌟 Streamlined Page Layout

The site now flows directly and seamlessly from the Hero to the project showcases:

1. **Page 1: Cinematic Video Hero Section (`#hero`)**
   - Fullscreen looping background video
   - Google Fonts: **Instrument Serif** + **Inter**
   - Clean top-left branding: **"ML CLUB®"**
   - Instant action buttons:
     - *Modules & Hardware*
     - *Online Projects*
     - *Upload Module Photo*
     - *Begin Journey*

2. **Page 2: Hardware & AI Modules Showcase (`#modules`)**
   - Directly visible right below the hero
   - **Interactive Multi-Photo Gallery**: Click any card or badge (e.g. *5 Photos • Open Gallery*) to open the Lightbox viewer
   - **Multi-Photo Upload Dropzone**: Upload up to 8 photos of student hardware with thumbnail previews (Protected by Core Access)

3. **Page 3: Online Projects Showcase (`#projects`)**
   - Category filtering: *All*, *Computer Vision*, *NLP & LLMs*, *Edge AI*, *Competitive ML*
   - Cards with **Live Demo** links and **GitHub Repository** links
   - Multi-photo screenshots gallery for every project

4. **Page 4: Student Registration (`#join`)**
   - Engineering student registration form (USN, Branch, Email, Phone, GitHub)
